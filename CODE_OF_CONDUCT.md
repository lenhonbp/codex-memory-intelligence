# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private project data, credentials, and vulnerability details.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect maintainer decisions and documented project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
